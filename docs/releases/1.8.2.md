## What's new

### Key achievements at the top of your tailored CV
The tailored CV now has a **Key achievements** section (in Swedish **Nyckelresultat**) between the profile and key skills: three to six results your CV itself states, such as a figure, something you delivered or something that got better. A duty is not a result, and if your CV has fewer than three results the section is left out rather than padded.

The same result isn't repeated in the same words further down. The top says what came of it, and the bullet under the job says how. A bullet that only repeats an achievement is taken out in code, and one that echoes an achievement but says more is named in the style notes so you can change it with Edit text. The fact check reads the achievements like every other line, so a result the ad wants but your CV never states is flagged.

### Two pages that look finished
When your CV has the material, the tailored CV uses more of it and aims at two full pages: more of your own detail for the jobs closest to the ad, and courses, certificates, projects and languages in full. It never fills space with anything your CV doesn't say, so a CV with one page of material stays one page.

The PDF is now laid out to fit its pages. A CV that spills a few lines onto page 2 is pulled back to one page, and one that ends early on page 2 gets more room between bullets and jobs so the page is properly used. Only the spacing changes, never the text.

### Unchanged
Searching, tracking, the cover letter, interview prep, the Mentor, the Market tab and both languages work as before.
