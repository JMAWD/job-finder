## What's new

### The place the ad says, not just the address
Some ads give the employer's office as their address and say where the job really is only in the text. One ad for a production engineer had a Göteborg address and the line "Location: Västerås, Sweden", so it turned up in Göteborg searches.

Job Finder now reads the place an ad states in its text, from a labelled line such as Location, Ort, Arbetsort or Placering, or a plain sentence such as "Tjänsten är placerad i Kungsbacka" or "The role is based in Gothenburg". When that names other municipalities than the address, the text wins. The ad is shown there with the address it gave in brackets, for example "Västerås (address given: Göteborg)", and it is left out of searches for the address's area. Places the text only mentions in passing, like "we have offices in Malmö and Lund", don't count, and an ad that says it's remote or anywhere in Sweden keeps its address.
