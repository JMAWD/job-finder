## What's new

### A dated list of the applications you've sent
My applications now has an export of every application you've sent, for your activity report to Arbetsförmedlingen or your own records. It gives one row per application, oldest first, with the date you applied, employer, role, municipality, ad link, how to apply, last day to apply, status, follow-up date and your notes. Download it as an Excel file or a CSV.

Pick This month, Last month or your own From and To dates, and the app shows how many applications fall in that range before you download. Leave the dates empty to get everything.

Only applications you've sent are included: Applied, Interview, Offer and Rejected. Saved and Not interested are left out.

### How the date is set
The app tracks a job once you give it a status, either from the menu on the job or with Mark as applied in step 2 after you've opened the application link. The first time a job becomes Applied, that day is saved as the date you applied. It stays the same when the job later moves to Interview, Offer or Rejected.

An application you marked as sent before the app saved the exact day gets the day its status last changed instead, and the export says so in its own column.

### Unchanged
Search, tracking, step 2, the Mentor, the Market tab and both languages work as before. Your applications are still stored only in your browser, so keep downloading a backup now and then.
